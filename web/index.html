    The plan needs a few numbers

    Without these counts, the plan can't use the evolutions below. Leave one blank if you don't know it: a family without a candy count gets no Rare Candy.

    Evolution items

    Stones, apples, lure modules and the like, from your item bag.

    Pokédex

    Species you've registered but don't hold any more. The Pokémon you hold count already.

    Never caught

    Species you've never caught. These win over a Pokémon read from storage by mistake.
